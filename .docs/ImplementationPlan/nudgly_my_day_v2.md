# Nudgly – My Day Feature (v2)

## Updated Philosophy
My Day balances:
- **Focus**: What you chose to do today
- **Awareness**: What you shouldn’t forget

---

## Layout Overview

### 1. Header
- Greeting + Date
- Mini Metrics:
  - Tasks progress (1/3)
  - Habits remaining (2)
  - Upcoming reminders (1)

---

### 2. Today’s Focus (Primary)
- 3–5 intentional tasks
- Clean, distraction-free
- Reorderable
- Satisfying completion

---

### 3. Habits (Quick Wins)
- Only habits due today
- Max 3–4 visible
- Show streak subtly
- Quick complete (+)

---

### 4. Upcoming Reminders
- Next 1–3 reminders
- Time-based visibility
- Soft UI styling (less visual weight)

---

### 5. Micro Progress
- “1 down, 2 to go”
- Lightweight progress bar

---

### 6. Plan My Day CTA
- Context-aware suggestions
- Encouraging tone

---

## Smart UX Enhancements

### Context Awareness
- Morning → habits focus
- Afternoon → reminders focus
- Evening → wrap-up

### Gentle Nudges
- “Quick win? Drink water”
- “You’ve got 1 habit left”

### Collapsible Sections
- Keep UI minimal by default

---

## Key Principle
> Show just enough to guide, never enough to overwhelm.

---

## Final Thought
Nudgly should feel like a calm, supportive assistant—not a task manager.
